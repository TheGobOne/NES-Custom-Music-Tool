# NES Custom Music Tool

Turns NES music (NSF / NSFe files) and SNES songs (SPC) into music packs for the Zelda 1 (ZORA) and Zelda 2 (Z2R) randomizers.

Everything runs in the page: nothing is uploaded anywhere. Open the site, pick the game, add your music files, normalize, and export a .json5 pack.

No game ROMs or music files are included in this repository.
